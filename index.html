About Us

Why SiteFlo exists and who it's for.

1. The Problem / Need

Design and finishing engineers in Egypt manage their projects without a simple tool to track costs, time and payments. As a result, they overspend on materials, projects run late, and they pay workers for extra time. Mistakes also damage their reputation with clients, and all of this reduces their profit.

2. The Target User

Independent design and finishing engineers and small finishing firms in Egypt who manage residential finishing projects (apartments and villas) for homeowners.

3. The Proposed Solution

SiteFlo is a simple project-management app that lets the engineer cost a project, set the service price, schedule the work week by week, and track progress, budget and client satisfaction in one place.

4. Why It Is Suitable

It targets the three business drivers directly. Cost: full cost build-up and budget tracking. Revenue: margin-based pricing and client reviews. Risk: warnings for missed tasks and payments, and a clear schedule. There are many alternatives to SiteFlo, but none are as accessible, simple, or tackle the problems as we do.
